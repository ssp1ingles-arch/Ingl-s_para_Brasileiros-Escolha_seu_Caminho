1 · O verbo TO BE

Unit 1am / is / are

  • My name is Lisa. I'm American. I'm from Chicago. I'm a student.
  • I'm not married. My father is a doctor and my mother is a journalist.
  • My favourite colour is blue. My favourite sports are football and swimming.
  • I'm cold. Can you close the window, please?
  • I'm 32 years old. My sister is 29.
  • My brother is very tall. He's a policeman.
  • John is afraid of dogs. · It's ten o'clock. You're late again.
  • Ann and I are good friends. · Your keys are on the table.
  • I'm tired but I'm not hungry.
  • Tom isn't interested in politics. He's interested in music.
  • Jane isn't at home at the moment. She's at work.
  • Those people aren't English. They're Australian.
  • It's sunny today but it isn't warm.
  • Thank you. That's very kind of you. · Look! There's Chris.
  • "Here's your key." "Thank you."

Unit 2am / is / are (perguntas)

  • "Am I late?" "No, you're on time."
  • "Is your mother at home?" "No, she's out."
  • Your shoes are nice. Are they new?
  • Is she at home? / Is your mother at home? (não "Is at home your mother?")
  • Where is your mother? Is she at home?
  • "Where are you from?" · "What colour is your car?"
  • "How old is Joe?" · How are your parents? Are they well?
  • Why are you angry? · "How much are these postcards?"
  • What's the time? · Who's that man? · Where's Jill? · How's your father?
  • "Are you tired?" "Yes, I am." / "No, I'm not."
  • "Is your friend English?" "Yes, he is." / "No, he isn't."

Unit 10was / were

  • Last year Rachel was 22, so she is 23 now.
  • When I was a child, I was afraid of dogs.
  • We were hungry after the journey but we weren't tired.
  • The hotel was comfortable but it wasn't expensive.
  • Was the weather good when you were on holiday?
  • Those shoes are nice. Were they expensive?
  • Why were you late this morning?
  • "Was Ted at work yesterday?" "No, he wasn't."
  • "Were Sue and Steve at the party?" "Yes, they were."

2 · Presente (simple & continuous)

Unit 3I am doing (present continuous)

  • Please be quiet. I'm working. (= agora)
  • Look at Sue! She's wearing her new hat.
  • The weather is nice at the moment. It's not raining.
  • (on the phone) We're having dinner now. Can you phone again later?
  • You can turn off the television. I'm not watching it.

Unit 4are you doing? (present continuous, perguntas)

  • "Are you feeling OK?" "Yes, I'm fine, thanks."
  • Why are you wearing a coat? It's not cold.
  • "What's Paul doing?" · "What are the children doing?"
  • Look, there's Sally! Where's she going?
  • Who are you waiting for? Are you waiting for Sue?
  • "Is Paul working today?" "No, it's his day off."
  • "Are your friends staying at a hotel?" "No, at my place."

Unit 5I do / work / like (present simple)

  • I work in a shop. My brother works in a bank.
  • Linda lives in London. Her parents live in Scotland.
  • It rains a lot in winter. · John has a shower every day.
  • The shops open at 9 o'clock and close at 5.30.
  • Tim works very hard. He starts at 7.30 and finishes at 8 o'clock in the evening.
  • The Earth goes round the Sun.
  • It costs a lot of money to stay at luxury hotels.
  • Sue always arrives at work early. (não "Sue arrives always")
  • I usually go to work by car but sometimes I walk.
  • Julia never eats breakfast. · Tom lives near us. We often see him.

Unit 6I don't ... (present simple negativo)

  • I drink coffee but I don't drink tea.
  • Sue drinks tea but she doesn't drink coffee.
  • You don't work very hard. · We don't watch television very often.
  • The weather is usually nice. It doesn't rain very often.
  • I don't like football. · He doesn't like football.
  • My car doesn't use much petrol. (não "My car don't use")
  • Sandra speaks Spanish but she doesn't speak Italian. (não "doesn't speaks")
  • Paula doesn't usually have breakfast.

Unit 7Do you ... ? (present simple, perguntas)

  • "What do you do?" "I work in a bank."
  • Do they like music? · Does he like music?
  • "Do you play tennis?" "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't."
  • "Do your parents speak English?" "Yes, they do."
  • "Does George work hard?" "Yes, he does."

Unit 8I am doing (agora) e I do (em geral)

  • Please be quiet. I'm working. (não "I work")
  • Tom is having a shower at the moment. (não "Tom has")
  • Take an umbrella with you. It's raining.
  • I work every day from 9 o'clock to 5.30.
  • Tom has a shower every morning. · It rains a lot in winter.
  • What do you usually do at the weekend?
  • I'm tired. I want to go home. (não "I'm wanting" — verbo de estado)
  • "Do you know that girl?" "Yes, that's Alice."
  • I don't understand. What do you mean?

3 · have got

Unit 9I have ... / I've got ...

  • I've got blue eyes. (ou I have blue eyes.)
  • Tim has got two sisters. (ou Tim has two sisters.)
  • Our car has got four doors.
  • Diane isn't feeling well. She's got a headache.
  • They like animals. They've got a horse, three dogs and six cats.
  • I've got a motor-bike but I haven't got a car.
  • Mr and Mrs Harris haven't got any children.
  • It's a nice house but it hasn't got a garden.
  • "Have you got a camera?" "No, I haven't."
  • "Has Ann got a car?" "Yes, she has."
  • They don't have any children. (= They haven't got any children.)
  • Does Ann have a car? (= Has Ann got a car?)
  • How much money do you have? (= How much money have you got?)

4 · Passado

Unit 11worked / got / went (past simple)

  • I clean my teeth every morning. This morning I cleaned my teeth.
  • Terry worked in a bank from 1986 to 1993.
  • Yesterday it rained all morning. It stopped at lunchtime.
  • We enjoyed the party last night. We danced a lot and talked to a lot of people.
  • I usually get up early but this morning I got up at 9.30.
  • We did a lot of work yesterday.
  • Caroline went to the cinema three times last week.
  • Jim came into the room, took off his coat and sat down.

Regulares: work → worked · study → studied · stop → stopped · plan → planned.

Unit 12I didn't ... Did you ... ? (past simple negativo e perguntas)

  • I don't watch television very often. · I didn't watch television yesterday.
  • Does she often go away? · Did she go away last week?
  • I played tennis yesterday but I didn't win.
  • We went to the cinema but we didn't enjoy the film.
  • "Did you see Joe yesterday?" "No, I didn't."
  • "Did Helen come to the party?" "Yes, she did."
  • "Did your parents have a good holiday?" "Yes, they did."

Unit 13I was doing (past continuous)

  • What were you doing at 11.30 yesterday? Were you working?
  • It was raining, so we didn't go out.
  • In 1985 we were living in Canada.
  • Today she's wearing a skirt, but yesterday she was wearing trousers.
  • I woke up early yesterday. It was a beautiful morning. The sun was shining.

Unit 14I was doing e I did

  • Jack was reading a book when the phone rang. (ação em curso + ação curta)
  • It wasn't raining when I got up.
  • I started work at 9 o'clock and finished at 4.30. At 2.30 I was working.
  • It was raining when we went out. (começou a chover antes de sairmos)
  • I saw Lucy and Steve this morning. They were waiting at the bus stop.
  • Jenny fell asleep while she was reading.

Unit 25I used to ...

  • When I was a child, I used to like chocolate.
  • I used to read a lot of books but I don't read much these days.
  • Liz has got short hair now but it used to be very long.
  • They used to live in the same street as us, so we often used to see them.
  • Ann used to have a piano but she sold it a few years ago.
  • When I was a child I didn't use to like tomatoes.
  • Where did you use to live before you came here?

5 · Present Perfect

Unit 15I have done (present perfect 1)

  • I've lost my passport. (= não consigo achá-lo agora)
  • We've bought a new car. (= temos um carro novo agora)
  • It's Rachel's birthday tomorrow and I haven't bought her a present.
  • Can I take this newspaper? Have you finished with it?

Unit 16I've just ... / already ... / not ... yet

  • It's only nine o'clock and Ann has already gone to bed.
  • "Have Diane and Paul arrived yet?" "Yes, they're here."
  • "Has Linda started her new job yet?" "No, not yet — next week."

Unit 17Have you ever ... ? (present perfect 3)

  • "Have you been to France?" (na sua vida)
  • I've been to Canada but I haven't been to the United States.
  • I've seen that woman before but I can't remember where.
  • How many times has Brazil won the World Cup?
  • "Has Ann ever been to Australia?" "Yes, once."
  • My mother has never travelled by air. · I've never ridden a horse.
  • I can't find Susan. Where has she gone? (= onde ela está agora?)
  • Oh, hello Susan! I was looking for you. Where have you been?

Unit 18-19How long have you ... ? · for / since / ago

  • Susan started her new job three weeks ago. (= três semanas antes de agora)
  • I had dinner an hour ago. · Life was very different a hundred years ago.
  • When did Jill arrive in Ireland? · How long has she been in Ireland?
  • John has been in hospital for three days. (não "since three days")
  • Mr and Mrs Kelly have been married since 1998.
  • It has been raining since I arrived.
  • They've been married for ten years.

Unit 20I have done (present perfect) e I did (past simple)

  • I saw Paula yesterday. (não "I have seen")
  • Where were you on Sunday afternoon? (não "Where have you been")
  • We didn't have a holiday last year. (não "We haven't had")
  • William Shakespeare lived from 1564 to 1616. He wrote many plays.
  • I have lost my key. · I lost my key last week.
  • Bill has gone home. · Bill went home ten minutes ago.
  • Have you seen Ann? · Did you see Ann on Saturday?

6 · Passiva

Unit 21is done / was done (passive 1)

  • Butter is made from milk. · Oranges are imported into Britain.
  • I am never invited to parties.
  • This house was built 100 years ago.
  • These houses were built 100 years ago.
  • When was the telephone invented?
  • We weren't invited to the party last week.
  • I was born in London in 1962. (não "I am born")
  • Where were you born?
  • The telephone was invented by Alexander Bell in 1876.
  • My brother was bitten by a dog last week.

Unit 22is being done / has been done (passive 2)

  • My car is at the garage. It is being repaired. (= alguém está consertando)
  • Some new houses are being built opposite the park.
  • The office is being cleaned at the moment.
  • My key has been stolen. (= alguém o roubou)
  • I'm not going to the party. I haven't been invited.
  • Has this shirt been washed?
  • The room is clean now. It has been cleaned.

Unit 24Verbos regulares e irregulares

  • I made a cake yesterday. (past simple)
  • I have made some coffee. (past participle — present perfect)
  • Butter is made from milk. (past participle — passiva)
  • Somebody broke this window last night.
  • Somebody has broken this window. · This window was broken last night.

7 · Futuro

Unit 26What are you doing tomorrow? (present continuous = futuro)

  • Andrew is playing tennis tomorrow. · I'm not working next week.
  • Alice is going to the dentist on Friday.
  • We're having a party next weekend. · Are you meeting Bill this evening?
  • What are you doing tomorrow evening?
  • I'm not going out tonight. I'm staying at home.
  • Are you going out tonight? (não "Do you go")
  • The train arrives at 7.30. · What time does the film finish? (horários)

Unit 27I'm going to ...

  • I'm going to buy some books tomorrow.
  • Sarah is going to sell her car.
  • I'm not going to have breakfast this morning. I'm not hungry.
  • What are you going to wear to the party tonight?
  • Are you going to invite John to your party?
  • Look at the sky! It's going to rain.
  • Oh dear! It's 9 o'clock and I'm not ready.

Unit 28-29will / shall

  • You can phone me this evening. I'll be at home.
  • We'll probably go out this evening.
  • I won't be here tomorrow. (= I will not be here)
  • Don't drink coffee before you go to bed. You won't sleep.
  • I think Diana will pass the exam. · I don't think it will rain this afternoon.
  • I'm tired. I think I'll go to bed early tonight.
  • It's a nice day. I think I'll sit in the garden.
  • "My bag is very heavy." "I'll carry it for you."
  • It's very warm in this room. Shall I open the window?
  • It's a nice day. Shall we go for a walk?
  • Where shall we go for our holidays this year?

8 · Modais

Unit 30might / may

  • I might go to the cinema this evening. (= é possível que eu vá)
  • Take an umbrella with you. It might rain.
  • Buy a lottery ticket. You might be lucky.
  • I might not go to work tomorrow. (= é possível que eu não vá)
  • Sue might not come to the party.
  • I may go to the cinema this evening. (= I might go)
  • May I ask a question? (= Can I ask?) · "May I sit here?"

Unit 31can and could

  • I can play the piano. My brother can play the piano too.
  • Sarah can speak Italian but she can't speak Spanish.
  • "Can you swim?" "Yes, but I'm not a very good swimmer."
  • I'm having a party next week but Paul and Jenny can't come.
  • When I was young, I could run very fast.
  • Before Anna came to Britain, she couldn't understand much English.
  • I was tired last night but I couldn't sleep.
  • Can you open the door, please? ou Could you open the door, please?
  • (in a shop) Can I have these postcards, please? ou Could I have ... ?
  • (on the phone) Hello, can I speak to Gerry, please?

Unit 32must / mustn't / needn't

  • The windows are very dirty. I must clean them.
  • It's a fantastic film. You must see it.
  • We must go to the bank today. We haven't got any money.
  • I must hurry. I mustn't be late. · I mustn't forget to phone Julia.
  • You mustn't touch the pictures. (proibição)
  • I needn't clean the windows. They aren't very dirty.
  • You needn't go to the bank today. I can give you some money.
  • I don't need to clean the windows. (= I needn't clean)
  • You mustn't go. You must stay here. (vs) You needn't go. You can stay here if you want.

Unit 33should / ought to

  • Tom should go to bed earlier. He goes to bed very late and he's always tired.
  • It's a good film. You should go and see it.
  • When you play tennis, you should always watch the ball.
  • You watch TV all the time. You shouldn't watch TV so much.
  • I think Carol should buy some new clothes.
  • I don't think you should work so hard.
  • Do you think I should buy this hat?
  • It's a good film. You ought to go and see it. (= you should go)

Unit 34I have to ...

  • I'll be late for work tomorrow. I have to go to the dentist.
  • Jill starts work at 7 o'clock, so she has to get up at 6.
  • You have to pass a test before you can get a driving licence.
  • I was late for work yesterday. I had to go to the dentist.
  • What time do you have to go to the dentist tomorrow?
  • Why did they have to leave the party early?
  • I'm not working tomorrow, so I don't have to get up early.
  • Ian doesn't have to work very hard. He's got an easy job.
  • We didn't have to wait very long for the bus.

Unit 35Would you like ... ? / I'd like ...

  • "Would you like some coffee?" "No, thank you."
  • "What would you like, tea or coffee?" "Tea, please."
  • Would you like to go for a walk?
  • What would you like to do this evening?
  • I'm thirsty. I'd like a drink.
  • (in a tourist office) I'd like some information about hotels, please.
  • I'd like to see the film on television this evening.
  • Do you like going to the cinema? (em geral) vs Would you like to go to the cinema? (agora)

9 · there is / it

Unit 36there is / there are

  • There's a big tree in the garden. · There's a good film on TV tonight.
  • "Excuse me, is there a hotel near here?" "Yes, there's one near the station."
  • We can't go skiing. There isn't any snow.
  • There are some big trees in the garden. · There are a lot of accidents on this road.
  • "Are there any letters for me today?" "No, there aren't."
  • This restaurant is very quiet. There aren't many people here.
  • How many players are there in a football team? · There are 11 players.
  • There's a lot of salt in this soup.

Unit 37there was / were · there has been · there will be

  • There was a good film on TV last night.
  • We stayed at a very big hotel. There were 1,250 rooms.
  • Were there any letters for me yesterday?
  • When I got home, I was hungry but there wasn't anything to eat.
  • Look! There's been an accident. (= there has been)
  • This road is very dangerous. There have been many accidents.
  • Do you think there will be a lot of people at the party?

Unit 38It ... (hora, dia, distância, tempo)

  • What time is it? · It's half past ten. · It's late. · It's time to go home.
  • What day is it? · It's Thursday. · It's 16 March.
  • It's three miles from our house to the city centre.
  • How far is it from London to Bristol? · It's a long way from here to the station.
  • It's raining. · It rains a lot here. · It's warm / hot / cold / cloudy / windy / sunny.
  • It's a nice day today.
  • It's nice to see you again. · It's impossible to understand her.
  • It wasn't easy to find your house.
  • It's raining again. (não "Is raining again")
  • Is it true that you are going away?

10 · Respostas curtas e concordância

Unit 39I am / I don't etc. (respostas curtas)

  • I haven't got a car but my sister has. (= my sister has got a car)
  • She isn't tired but he is.
  • My sister has got a car but I haven't.
  • "Are you and Jim working tomorrow?" "I am but Jim isn't."
  • I don't like hot weather but Sue does. (= Sue likes hot weather)
  • Sue works hard but I don't. (= I don't work hard)
  • "Did it rain yesterday?" "Yes, it did." / "No, it didn't."

Unit 40Have you? Are you? Don't you? (respostas de interesse)

  • "Bill can't drive." "Can't he? I thought he could."
  • "I'm not hungry." "Aren't you? I am."
  • "Sue isn't at work today." "Isn't she? Is she ill?"
  • "I speak four languages." "Do you? Which ones?"
  • "Tom doesn't eat meat." "Doesn't he? Does he eat fish?"
  • "Linda got married last week." "Did she? Really?"

Unit 41too / either · so am I / neither do I

  • Mary is a doctor. Her husband is a doctor too.
  • Bill doesn't watch TV. He doesn't read newspapers either.
  • "I'm happy." "I'm happy too." / "So am I."
  • "I'm not happy." "I'm not happy either." / "Neither am I."
  • "I enjoyed the film." "So did I."
  • "I can't cook." "Neither can I."
  • "I work in a bank." "So do I."
  • "I never go to the cinema." "Neither do I."

11 · Perguntas

Unit 43is it? have you? do they? (perguntas 1)

  • Where has David gone? (não "Where has gone David?")
  • When was the telephone invented?
  • What do you usually do at weekends?
  • "What does your brother do?" · "I broke my finger last week."
  • Where's John? Why isn't he here? (não "Why he isn't here?")
  • Why can't Paula come to the meeting tomorrow?
  • Why didn't you phone me last night?

Unit 44Who saw you? Who did you see? (perguntas 2)

  • Who lives in this house? (who = sujeito)
  • What happened? · What's happening?

  • Would you like to sit down? · The picture fell down.
  • The thief ran away. (ou ran off)
  • Ann has gone away for a few days.
  • Go away and don't come back!
  • Ann is away. She'll be back on Monday.
  • Turn over and look at the next page.

Unit 108put on your shoes / put your shoes on (phrasal verbs 2)

  • It was cold, so I put on my coat. · Here's your coat. Put it on.
  • I'm going to take off my shoes. · Your shoes are dirty. Take them off.
  • It was dark, so I turned on the light.
  • I switched on the light and switched off the television.
  • I stopped reading and put my book down.
  • I took my new sweater back to the shop.
  • I read the letter and then put it back in the envelope.

23 · Conjunções e orações

Unit 109and / but / or / so / because

  • I got home, had something to eat, sat down in an armchair and fell asleep.
  • Ann is at work, Sue has gone shopping and Chris is playing football.
  • Because the water wasn't clean, we didn't go swimming.
  • It was late and I was tired, so I went to bed.
  • I always enjoy visiting London, but I wouldn't like to live there because it's too big.

Unit 110When ...

  • When I get home this evening, I'm going to have a shower.
  • I can't talk to you now. I'll talk to you later when I have more time.
  • Please close the window before you go out. (não "before you will go")
  • Julia is going to live in our flat while we are away. (não "while we will be")
  • I'll stay here until you come back. (não "until you will come back")

Unit 111If we go ... / If you see ... (1ª condicional)

  • If you see Ann tomorrow, can you ask her to phone me?
  • If I'm late this evening, don't wait for me. (não "if I will be")
  • What shall we do if it rains? (não "if it will rain")
  • If I don't feel well tomorrow, I'll stay at home.
  • We're going to play tennis if it doesn't rain. (não "when it doesn't rain")

Unit 112If I had ... / If we went ... (2ª condicional)

  • If he had the money, he would buy a car.
  • I don't know the answer. If I knew the answer, I'd tell you.
  • It's raining, so we're not going out. We'd get wet if we went out.
  • Jane lives in a city. She wouldn't be happy if she lived in the country.
  • If you didn't have a job, what would you do? (mas você tem um emprego)
  • I'm sorry I can't help you. I'd help you if I could. (mas não posso)
  • If we had a car, we could travel more.
  • It's cold. If I were you, I'd put your coat on.
  • It would be nice if the weather were (or was) better.

Unit 113-114a person who ... / a thing that ... (orações relativas)

  • Do you remember the woman who was playing the piano at the party?
  • I met a woman who can speak six languages. (who = sujeito)
  • Jim was wearing a hat that was too big for him. (that = sujeito)
  • The bag that he is carrying ... ou The bag he is carrying ... (com ou sem "that")
  • Did you find the book you wanted? (ou the book that you wanted?)
  • The people we met were very nice. (ou The people who we met ...)
  • Everything I said was true.
  • The film we saw was very good. (não "The film we saw it was ...")
  • The hotel where we stayed was near the station.
